SERVICES

How a project runs, from your first message to launch.

  1. 01

    Talk

    We chat on WhatsApp about your business, your customers and what the site needs to do. I ask for your logo, photos and any text you already have.

    brief · goals · your content
  2. 02

    Design

    I start from what your customers look for first, like the menu, prices or admission details, and design each page around it. You see the design and ask for changes before I build anything.

    customer first · layout · your feedback
  3. 03

    Build

    I write the HTML, CSS and JavaScript by hand, add motion where it helps, and test every page on a phone as well as a laptop.

    hand-written code · GSAP · mobile first
  4. 04

    Launch

    I put the site live, check every link and WhatsApp button on a real phone, and walk you through the finished site.

    live link · checks · handover

FAQ

Questions clients ask

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages and features you need. Tell me about your business on WhatsApp and I will send you a price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly your logo, photos and text are ready. You get a timeline together with the price.

What do I need to prepare?

Your business name, your logo if you have one, photos of your products or place, and the information you want on the site. If you are missing something, we can work it out together.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You see the design before I build it, and we agree on rounds of changes before work starts.

Do you use templates or website builders?

No. I design each site for the business it belongs to and write the code by hand.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Most of your customers will open it on a phone, so I design for phones first and test on real ones.

Can customers order through the site?

Yes. On the KOBAB site, customers pick items from the menu and the order is sent to the shop on WhatsApp. I can set up the same for your business.
